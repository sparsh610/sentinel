Sentinel test corpus. Written for development. Do not treat as legal guidance.

AML-01  Customer Due Diligence

AML-01.1  Standard customer due diligence shall be applied before establishing a business
relationship, and shall comprise identification of the customer, verification of that identity
from an independent source, and identification of the beneficial owner where the customer is a
legal person.

AML-01.2  Simplified due diligence may be applied only where the relationship presents a
demonstrably low risk, and never where the customer is established in a high-risk third
country.

AML-02  Enhanced Due Diligence

AML-02.1  Enhanced due diligence shall be applied to any politically exposed person, to any
customer established in a high-risk third country, and to any relationship the risk assessment
scores above 70.

AML-02.2  Enhanced due diligence requires senior management approval before the relationship is
established, and the source of funds and source of wealth shall be established and recorded.

AML-03  Transaction Monitoring

AML-03.1  All transactions shall be screened against the monitoring scenarios. A transaction
that triggers a scenario shall raise an alert for investigation by an analyst.

AML-03.2  An alert shall be dispositioned within five business days. An alert that is not
dispositioned within that period shall be escalated to the team lead.

AML-04  Structuring

AML-04.1  Structuring is the division of a single transaction into several smaller transactions
for the purpose of avoiding a reporting threshold.

AML-04.2  Where three or more transactions by the same customer within seven calendar days
together exceed EUR 10,000, and each individually falls below it, the pattern shall be treated
as potential structuring and an alert raised.

AML-04.3  A potential structuring pattern that the analyst confirms shall be reported to the
Financial Intelligence Unit without delay, and in any event within 24 hours of confirmation.

AML-05  Reporting

AML-05.1  A suspicious activity report shall be submitted where there is knowledge, suspicion,
or reasonable grounds to suspect that funds are the proceeds of criminal activity.

AML-05.2  A suspicious activity report shall be approved by a senior approver before
submission. An analyst may draft a report but may not submit one.

AML-05.3  The customer shall not be informed that a report has been made. Tipping off is a
criminal offence.

AML-06  Record Keeping

AML-06.1  Due diligence records and transaction records shall be retained for five years from
the end of the business relationship or the date of the transaction, whichever is later.

AML-06.2  Records shall be retrievable within a reasonable period on request from a competent
authority.
