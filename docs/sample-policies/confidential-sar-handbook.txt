SAR-01  Handling a Suspicious Activity Report After Sign-off

SAR-01.1  Once a senior approver has signed off a suspicious activity report, it shall be filed
with the Financial Intelligence Unit within one business day of the sign-off.

SAR-01.2  The customer shall not be told, directly or by implication, that a report has been
filed or is being considered. Account restrictions applied after a report shall be explained to
the customer only in the terms agreed with the Money Laundering Reporting Officer.

SAR-02  Law-Enforcement Requests

SAR-02.1  A production order or a request from law enforcement concerning a customer under
investigation shall be routed to the Money Laundering Reporting Officer and shall not be
recorded in the case note an analyst can see.

SAR-02.2  Where law enforcement asks the bank to keep an account open, the request shall be
confirmed in writing before any monitoring threshold for that customer is changed.

SAR-03  Internal Watch List

SAR-03.1  Customers on the internal watch list shall have every alert escalated for senior
review, whatever the recommendation in the case note.

SAR-03.2  The watch list itself is CONFIDENTIAL and shall be shown to senior approvers only.
